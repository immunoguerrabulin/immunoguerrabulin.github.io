Lutrin

Sheet music reader for iPad. Free.

Read your PDF scores and parts, mark them with Apple Pencil, build setlists, and play concerts with hands-free page turns from a Bluetooth pedal. Practice with a metronome, tuner, recorder and practice log, all in one app.

  • PDF and scanned sheet music, with markings on layers you can show or hide
  • Setlists and Stage mode for performances, with half-page turns and repeats in playing order
  • Bluetooth and MIDI page-turn pedals
  • Metronome, tuner, recorder, practice log and piano keyboard
  • No account, no tracking, no data collected
