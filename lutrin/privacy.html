Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026

Short version: Lutrin collects no data about you. It has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not send your library to the developer, Jose Luis Guerra. Your music, markings, setlists and recordings stay on your iPad unless you choose to share or export them.

What stays on your iPad

Everything you put into Lutrin is stored only on your iPad: the PDFs and photos you import, your markings and layers, setlists, practice logs, audio tracks and recordings, and your settings. We (the developer) never receive any of it. Lutrin does not use iCloud sync.

If your iPad backs itself up to iCloud or to a computer, your Lutrin library is included in that backup, handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Lutrin's own backups are files saved on your iPad (in Files › Lutrin › Backups), and they go only where you copy them.

Permissions Lutrin asks for

  • Microphone: used by the tuner to hear your instrument and by the recorder to record your practice. Sound is processed on the iPad. Recordings are saved on the iPad and are shared only if you share them.
  • Camera: used only when you scan a paper part into your library. The scanned pages are saved on the iPad.
  • Bluetooth: used only to connect the MIDI page-turn pedals you choose in Settings › Pedals.

You can turn each permission off at any time in the iPad's Settings › Privacy & Security. Lutrin keeps working without them, except for the feature that needs that permission.

Password-protected PDFs

If you choose “Remember in Keychain” when opening a password-protected PDF, Lutrin keeps its password in this iPad's keychain so it can open the part without asking again. The saved password is deleted when you delete the part for good.

Crash and hang reports

Off by default. If you turn on Settings › Privacy › "Keep crash and hang reports", Lutrin keeps Apple's diagnostic reports about crashes and hangs on your iPad. They are never sent automatically. You can choose to e-mail them to us to help fix a problem, and then we use them only for that purpose.

Sharing and exporting

When you share or export a part, a setlist, a recording or a backup, Lutrin creates a file and hands it to the destination you pick (for example Files, AirDrop, Mail or Messages). What happens to it there is up to you and that service.

External websites and cloud files

If you open an external website, such as IMSLP or a support page, or import a file stored in iCloud or another cloud service, the browser or file provider handles that connection. Those services operate under their own privacy policies.

Children

Lutrin is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. We will not start collecting data without saying so here first and updating the App Store privacy details.
