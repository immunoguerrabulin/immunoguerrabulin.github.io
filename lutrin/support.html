Lutrin Support

Questions, problems or ideas? E-mail Jose Luis Guerra at joseguerra587@gmail.com. Please include your iPad model and iPadOS version (Settings › General › About), and if something went wrong, what you did just before.

Getting started

How do I add music?

In the Library, tap + to import PDFs or photos from Files, or to scan a paper part with the camera. You can also open a PDF in Files or Mail and share it to Lutrin. To try the app with nothing of your own, tap Add Sample Score in the Library.

How do I turn pages?

Tap the right edge of the page to go forward and the left edge to go back. You can also use a Bluetooth page-turn pedal; see "Pedals" below.

Markings

How do I write on the music?

Open a piece in Rehearse and use Apple Pencil. Choose a pen, highlighter or eraser from the palette, or add stamps such as bowings and fingerings. Markings can go on separate layers, so you can show or hide, for example, your teacher's markings.

Setlists and Stage

How do I play a concert from a setlist?

Make a setlist in Setlists, add your pieces in order, then tap Stage. Stage hides everything except the music, and pages turn with a tap or a pedal. Tap the middle of the page to show the button that leaves Stage.

Can pages follow repeats and jumps?

Yes. Mark the repeats, D.S., D.C., codas and cuts once, and Stage turns the pages in playing order.

Pedals

For pedals in keyboard mode, pair the pedal in the iPad's Settings › Bluetooth, then check it in Lutrin › Settings › Pedals. For Bluetooth MIDI pedals, connect in Lutrin › Settings › Pedals › Bluetooth MIDI Devices…, then assign actions in MIDI Pedals.

Practice

Open a piece in Practice for the metronome, tuner, recorder, practice log and piano keyboard. The tuner and recorder ask for microphone access the first time.

Backups and moving to a new iPad

Lutrin makes backups of your whole library automatically and keeps them in Files › Lutrin › Backups. Copy a backup to iCloud Drive, a computer or a USB drive to keep it safe. On a new iPad, install Lutrin, put the backup file into Files › Lutrin › Backups, then choose it in Lutrin › Settings › Backups and tap Restore.

Privacy

Lutrin has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking, and does not send your library to the developer. See the Privacy Policy for details, including external websites and cloud file imports.
